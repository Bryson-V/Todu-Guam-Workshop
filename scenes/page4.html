Healthy Habits

Maintain a healthy lifestyle by eating nutritious foods, staying physically active, getting enough sleep,
managing stress, and having regular health checkups. Healthy daily habits help prevent illness, improve
overall well-being, and support a longer, healthier life.

Wash your hands regularly with soap and clean water for at least 20 seconds. Proper handwashing helps remove germs, prevents the spread of illnesses, and keeps you and those around you healthy. If soap and water are unavailable, use an alcohol-based hand sanitizer.
Limit or avoid alcohol and do not smoke. These habits can increase the risk of serious health problems, including heart disease, cancer, and lung disease. Choosing a healthier lifestyle helps protect your body and improves your overall well-being.
Choose a balanced diet with plenty of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins. Eating healthy provides your body with essential nutrients, boosts your immune system, and helps prevent chronic diseases while keeping you energized.
Engage in regular physical activity to maintain a healthy weight, strengthen your heart, and reduce the risk of chronic diseases. Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity exercise per week.
Prioritize quality sleep by maintaining a consistent sleep schedule and creating a restful environment. Adequate sleep is essential for physical and mental health, supporting immune function, cognitive performance, and emotional well-being.
Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water throughout the day. Proper hydration is crucial for maintaining bodily functions, regulating temperature, and supporting overall health.
