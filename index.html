A resident receives a blood sugar screening during a Guam Regional Medical City health outreach at the Dededo Senior Citizens Center. The event provided free health screenings and medical services for Guam residents from the Federated States of Micronesia, helping improve access to preventive care and health education.
The DPHSS Mobile Health Clinic brings free health screenings, preventive care, and health education directly to Guam's communities, improving access to care for underserved residents and connecting them with ongoing health services.
Todu Guam Foundation’s Prevention and Education program promotes proactive well-being through “Let’s Talk” forums and preventive health services. The program encourages positive lifestyle changes, covers broad health topics, and holds talks across the community.
The Todu Guam Mobile Care Clinic brings free primary and preventive health services directly to underserved communities, reducing transportation barriers and improving access to care. Services include medical checkups, immunizations, vision and oral screenings, physical exams, and other basic health care at no cost.
The Guam Medical Association held a homeless health outreach at the Salvation Army Light House Recovery Center, providing free health consultations and blood pressure screenings to support the community's health needs.
The Department of Public Health and Social Services hosted a Back-to-School Immunization Outreach, providing free routine vaccinations for eligible children to help families prepare for the school year and improve access to preventive health care.
